Mechano-sensing channels PIEZO1 and PIEZO2 regulate mitral valve serotonin-mediated homeostasis and pathophysiology

Stachelek S.J., Hauber E.J., Yang X., Laurence D.W., Rusinkevich V., Lu P., et al.

Cardiovascular Research · cvag190 · Published online September 3, 2026 (accepted manuscript)

Read paper ↗BibTeX ↓
Research summary

Human mitral valve samples and cultured interstitial cells revealed roles for PIEZO1 and PIEZO2 in serotonin regulation. In cell culture, activating PIEZO1 lowered SLC6A4 expression and increased HTR2B expression, serotonin levels, and protein synthesis. Under cyclic tension, silencing either channel reduced the decrease in SLC6A4 expression and the increase in collagen synthesis. These findings link mechanical signaling to pathways involved in mitral valve remodeling.
