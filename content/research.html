Valve cell mechanobiology

How do valve cells respond to altered mechanics?

I use human mitral valve endothelial and interstitial cells to compare responses under controlled mechanical conditions. These models allow me to study the cellular changes associated with mitral regurgitation.

PIEZO1 and serotonin

How does force sensing affect cell signaling?

I combine gene perturbation with molecular assays to examine the relationships among PIEZO1 activity, serotonin transport, HTR2B signaling, and matrix remodeling.
