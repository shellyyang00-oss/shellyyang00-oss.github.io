Research

How do forces shape
heart valve disease?

My research investigates how mechanical forces alter serotonin signaling and extracellular matrix remodeling in mitral regurgitation. I focus on the mechanosensitive channel PIEZO1, the serotonin transporter (SERT/SLC6A4), and serotonin receptor 2B (HTR2B) in human mitral valve cells.

I combine controlled mechanical stimulation with gene perturbation and molecular assays in human valve endothelial and interstitial cells. These experiments test how force sensing changes serotonin regulation and the cellular programs involved in matrix remodeling.

Valve cell mechanobiology

How does mechanical stimulation change valve cell behavior?

I use human mitral valve endothelial cells (MVECs) and interstitial cells (MVICs) to study responses to mechanical stimulation. Comparing these responses helps identify cellular changes associated with mitral regurgitation.

PIEZO1 and serotonin

How does PIEZO1 activity affect serotonin regulation?

I combine siRNA knockdown with RNA and protein assays to test how PIEZO1 activity relates to SERT/SLC6A4 and HTR2B. I then examine how changes in serotonin regulation relate to matrix remodeling.
