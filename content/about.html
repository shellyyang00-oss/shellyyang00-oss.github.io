Ph.D. student in Bioengineering

Robert J. Levy Laboratory · University of Pennsylvania

I study how mechanical forces change the behavior of mitral valve cells and contribute to tissue remodeling.

My work focuses on PIEZO1, serotonin transport, and HTR2B signaling in mitral regurgitation. I combine human valve cell models, controlled mechanical stimulation, and molecular assays to understand how force sensing affects the extracellular matrix.
